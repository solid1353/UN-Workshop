## Naruto's ↑↑○ face

The first time Naruto's ↑↑○ cutscene plays, his eyes and mouth draw well
below his head, near the ground, while the rest of him is in the air. Later
plays draw them on his head. NA2 and UN5 both do it. The opponent's face in
the same cutscene, Sasuke's in the case seen, draws on his head.
